Once we know the MAC address we can scan the ARP packets for responses and even include the attacker's MAC to see where he sent gratuitous packets to with 

`arp.opcode == 2 and arp.src.hw_mac == "MAC address of the attacker"`, this shows ARP responses. We can also tailor this more by adding the `arp.src.proto_ipv4` of the gateway.

1. Gratuitous ARP Response of the Attacker

3. A tailored gratuitous response that is tied to the IP of the gateway 

so the whole filter becomes 
`arp.opcode == 2 && arp.src.hw_mac == "attacker mac" && arp.src.proto_ipv4 == "gateway ip"`
