# Threat Intel Classifications 
1. Strategic intel - `What threats matter and what should we priotritize?
	- High-level intelligence that looks into the organisation's threat landscape and maps out the risk areas based on trends, patterns and emerging threats that may impact business decisions. An example is an annual ransomware trends report predicting a shift to data-wiping extortion in healthcare.
2. Tactical Intel - `How do attackers carry out their attack?`
	- Assessments of adversaries' behaviours through analysis of tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs). This can be in the form of Advisory notes, such as detailing new T1059.005 (Visual Basic) abuse in malspam.
3. Operational Intel - `Who is targeting whom, why, and what do we know about this campaign?` - A group targeting a specific sector for extortion
	-  Campaign-specific details about the motives and intent to perform an attack. This is useful for understanding the critical assets available in the organisation (people, processes, and technologies) that may be targeted.
4. Technical Intel - `What specific indicators can we use for this block`
	- Atomic indicators and artefacts such as IPs and hashes related to an attack.
